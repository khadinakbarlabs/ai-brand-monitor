# AI Brand Monitor privacy policy

Publisher: Khadin Akbar. Effective date: 2026-10-11.

AI Brand Monitor contains skills and supporting documents. It has no separate plugin-hosted service, analytics beacon or hidden telemetry. It guides the user's local agent and independently installed official Apify CLI.

For a user-approved collection, the CLI sends the selected company/product brand, domain, aliases, competitor brands, questions, platforms, and optional citation topics/page URLs to the publisher's existing Apify Actors. Those Actors query their configured AI services, including OpenAI, Perplexity, Google and Anthropic, using their configured provider routes. Apify stores run inputs, outputs and execution metadata in the user's account. These services process data under their own policies and account settings. This plugin does not promise a vendor retention period, model training exclusion or automatic deletion.

The plugin does not ask for credentials in chat, read login files, collect customer records or send conversation bodies, host memory or unrelated files. Authentication stays with the official CLI. Personal reputation analysis and sensitive personal data are outside the intended workflow. Users should keep prompts limited to business facts and public buyer questions.

When requested, campaign context, corrections, evidence, reports and actions are saved locally in the selected folder. They remain until the user removes them. Apify data remains subject to the user's account controls and the applicable provider policies. External report delivery, webhooks or publishing are not enabled by default; the package does not send them automatically.

Users can disable their schedules in Apify and manage or delete cloud storage through their account controls. They can remove their local campaign folder and uninstall the skills independently. Deleting a local report does not delete Apify or provider data. Do not include private prompts, reports or credentials in public support issues. See [support](SUPPORT.md) for general questions and [private security reporting](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Relevant policies: [Apify privacy](https://apify.com/privacy), [OpenAI privacy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), [Perplexity privacy](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/legal/privacy-policy), [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Anthropic privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
